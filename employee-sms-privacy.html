Tennessee Tepees Employee SMS

SMS Privacy Notice

Effective October 5, 2026

This notice covers the optional employee SMS program operated by Tennessee Tepees (Parkside Tepees). It does not enroll you in texts.

Information used by the program

We use your mobile number, messages you send or receive, message timestamps and delivery information, and your opt-in or opt-out keywords and timestamps. Our messaging provider, Twilio, maintains messaging logs as part of providing the service.

How we use it

We use this information to deliver and respond to employee operational communications, record and honor your SMS preferences, provide support, and troubleshoot delivery. The program does not send marketing messages. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Service providers and sharing

Authorized personnel and service providers involved in delivering or supporting this program may process this information for those purposes. We do not sell mobile numbers or share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Retention and access

We retain program records as needed to operate and support employee communications, honor opt-outs, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations. Access is limited to personnel and providers who need it for these purposes.

Your choices and requests

SMS participation is voluntary and is not a condition of employment. Reply STOP to cancel program texts. Ask your manager to arrange staff app or email communications instead.

For questions about your records, access or correction requests, or a request to delete program information, email gavin@tntepees.com. Some records may need to be retained to honor your opt-out or meet recordkeeping obligations.
