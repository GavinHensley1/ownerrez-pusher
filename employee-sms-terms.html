The program

Tennessee Tepees (Parkside Tepees) offers recurring automated operational text messages to invited employees. Messages may include operational questions, complaint and backup alerts, draft-approval requests, and staff reminders. This is not a StayDeck guest or marketing program.

Voluntary participation

After reading these terms and the SMS Privacy Notice, text START from your own mobile phone to (865) 381-7523 to opt in to Tennessee Tepees Employee SMS at that mobile number.

Consent is not a condition of employment. If you do not want texts, ask your manager to arrange staff app or email communications. Using the staff app, providing your phone number for work, or calling the transcription number does not by itself enroll you in SMS. Opting in does not grant staff app access.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Your mobile carrier's plan determines any charges for sending or receiving messages.

Stop, rejoin or get help

  • Reply STOP to cancel future program texts. You may receive a confirmation of your opt-out.
  • Text START from your own mobile phone to opt in again after reviewing the current program details.
  • Reply HELP for support or email gavin@tntepees.com.

Delivery and important communications

Message delivery depends on your carrier and service availability and is not guaranteed. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Do not use this program for emergencies. Use your established workplace contact method when a matter needs urgent attention or a message does not arrive.

Tell your manager if your mobile number changes so operational messages are not directed to an outdated number.
