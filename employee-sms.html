Tennessee Tepees Employee SMS

An optional way for invited employees to receive and respond to operational messages from Parkside Tepees.

SMS registration is pending. Wait until your manager confirms this line is activated before sending START. Operational texts begin only after approval, your opt-in, and management verification of your employee identity and actual START consent record.

What you can expect

Messages may include operational questions, complaint and backup alerts, requests to review message drafts, and staff reminders. Message frequency varies with work activity.

This program is for invited employees only. It is not a StayDeck guest program, and it does not send marketing messages. This number also receives staff transcription calls; calling it does not enroll you in SMS.

Choose whether to receive texts

Read the SMS Privacy Notice and SMS Terms. If you want to enroll, text START from your own mobile phone to (865) 381-7523.

By sending START, you agree to receive recurring automated operational SMS messages from Tennessee Tepees Employee SMS at that mobile number. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for support.

Consent is not a condition of employment. You can decline or stop texts and arrange staff app or email communications with your manager instead.

Open SMS app with START

This opens your messaging app; it does not send a message. Review the recipient and press Send only if you choose to opt in. You can also enter the number and START manually.

Manage your choice

STOP
Cancel future program texts. You may receive a confirmation of your opt-out.
START
Opt in, or rejoin after opting out, from your own mobile phone.
HELP
Request program support. You can also email us below.

Enrollment does not grant access to the staff app. SMS delivery is not guaranteed; do not use this program for emergencies.

Questions or another way to stay in touch?

Contact your manager or email gavin@tntepees.com for help, privacy requests, or an alternative communication method.
