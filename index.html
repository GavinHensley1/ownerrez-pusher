The on/off for the whole guest-messaging system. When ON, the engine drafts a reply to each guest message, texts it to you for approval, and sends your approved reply to the guest. When OFF, nothing is sent to guests.

🤖 Auto-message (hands-off replies)

Auto-send answers — the engine replies to guests itself instead of staging every message for approval
OFF
When ON: if the engine knows the answer it sends it to the guest automatically; if it doesn’t, it sends the guest a short “let me check with my manager” holding note and texts Victor for the fact. Victor only supplies the fact — the engine drafts a reply and texts it back to him, and nothing reaches the guest until he replies “Q# yes”. Pure “thank you” messages get no reply. Every answer it sends is queued below for your review before it’s trusted as a saved fact. When OFF: the classic flow — every reply waits in the approval queue for your YES/NO. (Still requires the Messaging master switch ON to actually send.)

🧠 Learned facts (pending your review)

New answers the engine sent, or facts Victor provided, are queued here before they’re saved into the knowledge base — nothing is trusted for future messaging until you approve it. Edit the wording if needed, then Approve to save it as a fact, or Reject to discard.

✅ Approvals — answer guest questions & review decisions ▸

Approval queue — guest questions awaiting Victor

When a question isn’t confidently answered by the knowledge base, it’s queued here and (once the SMS number is live) texted to Victor for a YES/NO. You approve by texting back (reply the Q-label with yes/no); approving sends the answer to the guest and saves it to the knowledge base. This list is read-only. Anything left undecided for 24 hours is automatically rejected (nothing is sent).

✅❌ Approved & rejected — what the AI recommended & your decisions

Every guest question the AI flagged: what it recommended, whether you approved it (and what was actually sent) or rejected it, and why. For rejected ones you can add or edit the reason — it teaches the assistant so it stops repeating the mistake.
